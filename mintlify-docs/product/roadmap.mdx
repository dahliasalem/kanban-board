## Not yet scoped

<Accordion title="Presence (who's currently viewing/editing a board)">
  Likely needs Firebase Realtime Database alongside Firestore for ephemeral, high-frequency state — Firestore is a poor fit for presence pings. Not yet scoped in detail.
</Accordion>

## Planned enhancements

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cross-board / global search">
    Search for cards across every board you're a member of. Firestore has no native full-text search, so a real version needs a dedicated search service (Algolia/Typesense) rather than per-board client-side filtering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Version history & restore">
    View a card's past edits and roll back to an earlier version, guarded by a version-number concurrency check so a restore can't silently clobber a concurrent edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Image & video attachments">
    Upload media to a card, stored in Firebase Storage with a reference on the card document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Card metadata: due dates and assignees">
    Opens the door to derived features like overdue badges or per-person workload counts later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Testing & monitoring">
    Unit tests for concurrency-critical logic (fractional-index generation, transaction retry behavior), Firestore security-rules tests, and production error/performance monitoring once there's real usage.
  </Step>
</Steps>
